About IIIT Kota

The Indian Institute of Information Technology, Kota (IIIT Kota), is an Institute of National Importance, established in 2013. It operates under a public-private partnership (PPP) model involving the Ministry of Education (MoE), the Government of India, the Government of Rajasthan, and industry partners. IIIT Kota is currently mentored by MNIT Jaipur and now functions from its permanent campus located in Ranpur, Kota. The campus spans 100.37 acres, allocated by the Government of Rajasthan since 2021.
